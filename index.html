Understanding how an application works is the beginning. Understanding where its assumptions fail is the research.

I’m imperror — A.majeed, a Bug Bounty Hunter and Cybersecurity Researcher specializing in Web Application Security.

My work combines manual testing, application behavior analysis, and vulnerability research. I focus on authorization, business logic, and the trust boundaries that shape modern web applications.

I have discovered vulnerabilities across major and local organizations, spanning different security categories and including high-severity findings. Additional reports are undergoing review and validation.

Understand the behavior. Validate the impact. Disclose responsibly.
